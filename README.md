# Awesome Medical Marketing [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of resources, tools, guides and benchmarks for marketing medical and healthcare practices — clinics, doctors, dentists, aesthetic and hair clinics, and treatment centers.

Marketing a clinic is not like marketing any other business: patients decide differently, healthcare advertising is regulated, and both search engines and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI) are changing how patients find care. This list gathers the resources that actually help.

Maintained by the team at [Medical Marketing](https://medicalmarketing.digital/) — a healthcare-only marketing agency (10+ years, Google Partner). Contributions welcome.
